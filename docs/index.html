AI for Notion, on the subscription you already have.

A floating assistant for the Notion desktop app that runs on your own Claude or ChatGPT sign-in. It reads your pages, writes and edits them in place, and keeps your calendar. No API keys, no Notion AI add-on, and nothing sent anywhere else.

The first launch looks blocked. It isn't. The app is signed but not notarized by Apple, so macOS cannot name the publisher. Right-click the app → Open, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Windows: More info → Run anyway. One time only.

Needs macOS 13 or later, Windows 10 or later, or a 64-bit Linux.

Set up in three steps

About ten minutes, most of it waiting on downloads. The setup screen inside the app walks you through the same three, with a green badge on each as it is done.

  1. Open the app

    It starts on its setup screen. Press ⌘⇧Space (Ctrl⇧Space on Windows) any time to bring it back.

  2. Connect the AI you already pay for

    Pick Claude or ChatGPT. The Install button opens a terminal that installs Claude Code or the Codex CLI; Sign in runs the login with your subscription. Oracle notices when each finishes.

  3. Connect Notion

    Create an internal integration at notion.so/profile/integrations, paste its secret (it is checked as you paste), then share your top-level pages with it in Notion: ••• → Connections. Sharing a page shares everything under it. This is the step people miss, and the app says so if it can see no pages.

Then try these, in order

The empty chat offers the same five. Each proves a different part of the setup, so the first one that fails tells you what to fix. Every change lands in the app's Changes list with an Undo.

  • 1What page am I looking at?hotkey, window, sign-in, search
  • 2Summarize this page in three bulletsreading content
  • 3What's on my calendar this week?calendar permission
  • 4Create a page under this one called Oracle Test, with a short outlinewriting
  • 5Add a to-do list of three things to the end of Oracle Testediting in place

Prefer the browser?

The browser extension puts Notion AI's selection menu, ⌘J at the cursor and a chat inside notion.so, on an Anthropic or OpenAI API key (pay per use, separate from a subscription). Unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, and Load unpacked.

Privacy

Your Notion content goes to the AI account you connected, Anthropic or OpenAI, and nowhere else. There is no Notion Oracle server and no telemetry. The integration secret and settings stay in a file on your machine; calendar access never leaves it. More.
