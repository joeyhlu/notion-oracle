Optional. Without it Oracle works on the page you have open. With it, Oracle can search your workspace, read other pages, create pages, add calendar entries and fill database columns.

  2. Copy its Internal Integration Secret and paste it below.
  3. In Notion, open a top-level page, choose ••• → Connections, and add Oracle. Pages under it come along.
